# User Guide

Help for people who use BilltUp.

- [Getting Started](./GETTING_STARTED.md): sign up, get the app, set up your business, send your first invoice, and what happens when the trial ends.

## Where to do what

| Task | Where |
|------|-------|
| Create and send invoices and estimates | Mobile app |
| Add and edit customers | Mobile app |
| Take payments and issue refunds | Mobile app |
| Sign up | Website |
| Choose, change, or cancel a plan | Website (Settings → Account) |
| Connect Stripe or Square | Website (Settings → Payment Settings) |
| Business profile, saved items, email notifications | Website (Settings) |
| Custom Branding (Premium) | Website (Settings → Custom Branding) |
| Sales analytics (Premium) | Website (Analytics) |
| Delete your account | Website (Settings → Account), the app, or email support |

## Plans

- **Free trial:** 14 days, no credit card, every Premium feature.
- **Basic:** $4.99/month. Up to 10 invoices per month and up to 10 customers.
- **Premium:** $9.99/month. Unlimited invoices and customers, plus analytics, Custom Branding, and invoice templates.

If the trial ends and you haven't chosen a plan, your account is read-only. Nothing is deleted.

## Your customers

Your customers don't need a BilltUp account. They get links that open on billtup.com:

- **Pay an invoice:** a secure checkout from Stripe or Square.
- **Approve or reject an estimate.**
- **Sign a document** with a finger or mouse.
